# Product

## Purpose

`sites.arturcarletto.com` is the commercial presence for website development, landing pages, demonstrations, prospecting, and future prospect-specific demos. It presents Artur Carletto directly as a Software Engineer and turns qualified business visits into WhatsApp conversations. The root domain `arturcarletto.com` remains reserved for Artur's broader professional presence.

## Audience

Small and medium businesses in Joinville and surrounding Santa Catarina, especially companies where each new lead has meaningful commercial value. The commercial subdomain remains personal: the visitor contracts Artur directly, not a fictional agency.

## Positioning

Software engineering applied to useful digital products, clear websites, and conversion-oriented experiences. The service is not sold as decoration: credibility, offer clarity, mobile usability, performance, and a short path to contact are core outcomes.

## Primary journey

1. A prospect receives a direct introduction and opens `sites.arturcarletto.com`.
2. The home establishes who Artur is and the quality of his work.
3. `/sites` explains the commercial offer.
4. `/modelos` or a selected concept demonstrates concrete quality.
5. A qualified prospect may receive an explicitly targeted `/demo/<slug>` page.
6. The visitor starts a WhatsApp conversation with Artur.

## Routes

- `/`: professional positioning, capabilities, selected concepts, process, and conversion.
- `/sites`: commercial website and landing page offer.
- `/sobre`: professional approach, principles, and working style.
- `/modelos`: transparent gallery of demonstration concepts.
- `/modelos/climatizacao`: high-conversion HVAC concept.
- `/modelos/moveis-planejados`: editorial custom furniture concept.
- `/modelos/estetica-automotiva`: dark premium detailing concept.
- `/modelos/odontologia`: warm, trustworthy dental concept.
- `/demo/<slug>`: optional, non-indexed demonstration prepared for one prospect; no pages exist until explicitly approved and registered.

## Business rules

- Demonstrations are not client work and must never imply otherwise.
- No unverified personal information or fabricated social proof appears.
- No prices are shown in v1.
- WhatsApp is the primary commercial contact; e-mail and LinkedIn are secondary.
- GitHub remains hidden until Artur explicitly defines it for this commercial experience.
- Demonstration forms and CTAs must clearly indicate their conceptual behavior.
- Prospect-specific demos stay out of navigation and the sitemap, use only verified or authorized information, and never imply an existing commercial relationship.
